Executive System Architecture & Technical Specifications

Architectural blueprint for the autonomous enterprise accounts payable validation platform.

1. Why Hybrid Agentic + Deterministic Architecture?

In enterprise finance, LLMs hallucinate calculations and cannot be trusted for financial balance arithmetic. Therefore, we strictly enforce a hybrid division of labor:

  • Multimodal AI (Gemini 3.8 Flash): OCR parsing, visual document understanding, and semantic vector discovery for unstated POs.
  • 100% Deterministic Python Code: Line-item math, tax separation, cumulative PO drawdowns, BEC bank wire verification, and dual-gate tolerance formulas.

2. Core Guardrails & Financial Policy Logic

Dual-Gate Tolerance Conjunction:

Both ≤ 3.0% AND ≤ $250.00 must hold. Protects against percentage leakages on large POs and immaterial review on small bills.

Net-to-Net Tax Separation:

Separates statutory embedded sales tax before PO reconciliation, eliminating false variance warnings on tax additions.
